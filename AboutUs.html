About heart and Crumb Bakery

Heart and Crumb bakery was established in the 1990s to provide fresh
homemade baked goods to people around the local community.
The bakery strictly used ingredients that were farmed and foraged.
Including grains, fruits, and herbs.The heart and Crumb still continues to focus on quality, freshness.
It began as a small family business and grew through the traditional recipes they provide

Mission

Turning a simple scratch-made ingredient to ensure that every crust tells
a story and every crumb makes a way to your heart and feels like home.

Vision

To be the beloved heart of the community, craft authentic bakes that
bring people together through a shared love of simple food.

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Freshness
  • Tradition
  • Community

Our Story

From a small bakery serving farming communities Heart and Crumb has grown while,
maintaining its traditional aproach to baking and its connection with the local community
